How paper trading works

The service checks public data every minute, records hourly observations, and evaluates allocation every four hours. Fees and available order-book depth affect estimated trades. Pausing keeps virtual holdings and history; it does not sell assets. After an application restart, resume explicitly. Research results use estimated historical costs and are not a guarantee of future returns.
